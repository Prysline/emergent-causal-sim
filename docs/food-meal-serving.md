## Canonical truth and quantity

- Each ready Meal exists exactly once as `state.objects[id]`, with `meal.edibleMassGrams` the sole positive integer edible quantity. No `servingsRemaining`, mutable `handling.massKg` duplicate, `contents.food`, Food inventory, reverse `children[]`, or replacement Resource unit.
- `SimFood.serveMeal(state,sourceId,plateId,requestedGrams,newMealId)` checks an exposed source and a legal empty plate before any mutation. Partial serving creates one new Meal ID with `containedBy=plateId` and exact gram debit from the original; the original remains at its previous support/position. Full serving preserves the original Meal identity and moves its canonical placement to `containedBy=plateId`. Failed serving has no debit or new Object. The effective portion is bounded by current source grams and actual Object storage mass capacity in integer grams.
- A contained Meal has neither `position` nor `supportId`. Spatial effective location follows its plate. Only the plate may be `Agent.held`. `SimObjectHandling.cargoMassKg` projects fully edible grams / 1000 to kg; it is the shared capacity/load authority for Object storage and Resource carried-load. This does not claim a generic total-body or inedible-weight physical model.
- Historical Cooking `producedByEventId` remains on derived portions; the Engine emits one canonical `serveMeal` World Event per actual physical transfer, records `servedByEventId`, and links actual intake through placement and serving to Cooking completion. Individual successful intake ticks remain separately realized canonical Events; partial/failed preparation alone never decreases Hunger.

## Eligible plate, observed information and contact

An exposed serving plate is deliberately opt-in: ordinary `portable:true`, `objectContainment:true`, valid `objectStorage`, `canEatFrom:true` and `interactions.eatFrom.mode` being a legal reach mode, without Resource `contents`. It is an ordinary Object, not legacy `resourceContainer` / `servingDish`.

- A Meal inside such a plate may be visually observed only via the canonical Perception projection of the plate's current real position and sight. If the plate is held by the observer, local access is permitted; a plate held by another actor is not made remotely edible/observable through Meal visibility.
- Only this explicit Food case projects contained Meal `eatFrom` interaction via the plate's canonical Spatial geometry. Production `src/spatial-traversal.js` overrides the base `src/spatial.js` resolver; both must delegate to the same `SimFood.servingPlateForMeal` truth and the **original v1 integration** required the effective traversal owner to export `MEAL_PLATE_INTERACTION_VERSION='meal-plate-eat-from-v1'` (historical introduced-in evidence; current owner exports v2 below). All other contained Objects still have their prior pickup-only interaction and hidden-point availability.
- Visible World inventory never equals universal Agent knowledge. `eat` source selection uses same-tick actual visual availability or legal contact and reachable interaction, with execution-time revalidation.

## Actual action lifecycle

`eat.prepare` may choose an available physical serving plate when a visible, directly exposed Meal exists. It reuses canonical pickup, current movement geometry, reservations, held Object relation, nearby release and existing `toMeal → eatingMeal`. Serving v1 phases originally were `toMealDish → takeMealDish → toMealServe → serveMeal → toMealDining → placeMealDish → toMeal → eatingMeal`; from 11.79.1 a legally seated Human may additionally take `toMeal → toMealSeat → sitMeal → eatingMeal`. Their names are Action-local phases, not another `serveMeal` Action kind or world ledger.

- A dedicated plate is chosen only if the actor has same-tick canonical Perception visibility **or real pickup contact**, and the plate is currently reachable, unheld, unreserved and empty. A remote reachable but unseen plate is World truth, not Agent knowledge. Source and container legality are checked again at the actual serving tick.
- An actually committed portion remains in its plate on ordinary Action interruption. The existing cleanup/release mechanism preserves real Object location and clears reservations; no magic rollback.
- Carrying the plate is genuine `Agent.held` and has canonical carry geometry/load. Since B2 (11.83.0), `placeMealDish` uses the explicit bounded intentional placement phase described below; the earlier immediate `releaseHeldObjectNearby` behavior remains the cleanup/interruption contract, not the current intentional Meal placement consumer. Other residents may take or eat from a placed exposed plate through ordinary `eat`, without a new proactive delivery Intent.
- Per effective `eatingMeal` tick at most 50 g are consumed and only that Agent's private Hunger is relieved by 0.08 per ingested gram. Social speaking continues to pause consumption, listening does not. Legacy Resource `eatingPlate` and `transferResource` are intentionally distinct.

## Physical Meal seating contact correction (11.79.1)

Physical Meal `toMeal` now chooses a reachable current Furniture Slot for a Human only when the projected seated stance can legally contact that Meal through canonical `eatFrom`. The Action reserves the Slot, travels to a legal approach and rechecks Slot availability, sitting fit and Meal contact at settlement before the existing `sitOn()` and a causal `sitForMeal` Event. A directly exposed Meal and an already-served plate reuse the same path. No legal seat means genuine `eatFrom` standing intake with `standForMeal`, not invented reach.

`toMealDining` carries the real plate; `placeMealDish` commits its actual location (historically immediate `releaseHeldObjectNearby`, now B2 intentional placement below). Only that actual location can enable seated contact. The Spatial `supportReach/eatFrom` projection reuses current-Slot Surface contact and does not give Chairs an independent Table association or reach radius. `SimSpatial.MEAL_PLATE_INTERACTION_VERSION` now reports `meal-plate-eat-from-v2-seated-slot-contact`; the original introduced-in v1 evidence above remains historical. Food grams / serving / legacy Resource Food and unaffected subsystem generations are unchanged. Fine-grained seat and placement preferences remain deferred.

## B2 intentional nearby placement (11.83.0)

B2 Intentional Nearby Placement v5: Physical Meal Serving `eat.placeMealDish` keeps immediate legal contact placement first, otherwise selects from Action-local observed Furniture IDs using canonical support contact and finite traversal cost (initial budget 4, elapsed bound 12 ticks). Remote occupancy is not queried for selection; B1 intrinsic fit and actual capacity are checked separately, actual capacity only at contact. Retain only initial short-range observed candidate IDs (not feasibility); recompute approach each tick, reject failed targets, recheck current local contacts, then use canonical Floor fallback when exhausted. No route/feasibility cache, no new Intent, no cleanup/abort/accidental-drop migration, no B1 precision change. Other consumers remain unchanged. The existing placeMealDish event still cites the actual serving event and includes the committed placementPosition; subsequent eatFrom/seat decision uses the real placed plate. Legacy Resource eating and two-hand cargo unloading remain unchanged.
